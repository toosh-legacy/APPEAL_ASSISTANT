## Your deadline to appeal
After a Medicaid managed care plan sends a notice of adverse benefit determination, you have 60 calendar days from the date on that notice to file an appeal with the plan (42 CFR 438.402(c)(2)(ii)).

## Expedited appeals
For an expedited appeal, the State must set a timeframe for the plan's decision that is no longer than 72 hours after the plan receives the appeal, which can be extended in some cases (42 CFR 438.408(b)(3)).

## State fair hearing
After the plan decides your appeal, you can request a State fair hearing. Your State gives you no less than 90 and no more than 120 calendar days from the date of the plan's notice of resolution (42 CFR 438.408(f)(2)).
