## Your deadline to ask for reconsideration
If your Medicare Advantage plan denies a service or payment (an "organization determination"), you can ask the plan to reconsider. The request must be filed within 60 calendar days after you receive the written notice (42 CFR 422.582(b)). Count from the date on your notice to be safe.

## Expedited (fast) reconsideration
If waiting could seriously harm your health, you can ask for an expedited reconsideration. When the plan grants it, the plan must decide as fast as your health requires and no later than 72 hours after it gets the request, with some exceptions (42 CFR 422.590(e)).

## Independent review
If the plan keeps its denial in whole or in part, the issues still in dispute must be reviewed and resolved by an independent outside entity that contracts with CMS (42 CFR 422.592(a)).
