## Emergency care without prior approval
Plans that cover emergency services must cover them without requiring prior authorization, even when the provider is out of network (45 CFR 149.110(b)(1)).

## Out-of-network emergency care
For covered emergency services from an out-of-network provider or facility, cost sharing generally cannot be higher than it would be in network (45 CFR 149.110(b)).

## Out-of-network providers at in-network facilities
Similar protections apply to many non-emergency services from out-of-network providers at in-network facilities (45 CFR 149.120) and to out-of-network air ambulance services (45 CFR 149.130).
