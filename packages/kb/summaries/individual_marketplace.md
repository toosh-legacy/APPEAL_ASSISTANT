## Your deadline to appeal
Individual health insurance issuers must follow the federal internal claims and appeals rules (45 CFR 147.136(b)(3)), which give you at least 180 days after the denial notice to file an internal appeal (29 CFR 2560.503-1(h)(3)(i)). Count from the date on your notice to be safe.

## Your right to see your file
You can ask for copies of the documents, records and other information relevant to your claim, free of charge, and you can send written comments and evidence for the appeal (29 CFR 2560.503-1(h)(2)).

## External review
After a final internal denial, you can ask for an independent external review through your State's process or the federal process (45 CFR 147.136(c) and (d)). Under the federal process, the request must be filed within four months after you receive the final internal denial notice (45 CFR 147.136(d)(2)). The reviewer's decision binds the insurer.
