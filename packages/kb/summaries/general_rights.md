## The denial notice must explain itself
For employer plans, a denial notice must give the specific reason for the denial and refer to the plan provisions it is based on (29 CFR 2560.503-1(g)(1)). If the reason is unclear, you can ask the plan for the rule, guideline or criterion it relied on.

## Keep copies and send in writing
Send your appeal in writing, keep a copy of everything, and write down the date you sent it and how.
